# Five-Customer Validation Experiment — Run Book

**No software is to be written until this experiment is complete and the
decision rule says BUILD.**

## What this tests

One hypothesis: **that publicly sourced opportunities give NRS information they
did not already have AND would act upon.** Nothing else.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **`decision-rule.md`** | ⚠️ **Read first.** Critique of the proposed thresholds, the revised rule, and the lock to sign |

## Order of operations

1. Read `decision-rule.md`. **Sign the lock.** Do this before recruiting.
2. Print: 10 cards (folded), 5 capture sheets, 5 participant sheets.
3. Recruit 5 — **at least 3 must be involved in winning work**.
4. Run the interviews. One pack per person. Never two packs with one person.
5. Complete each capture sheet **in the room**.
6. Wait **14 days**. Do not chase. Record who contacted a counterparty.
7. Run `post-interview-analysis.md`. Validity checks first.
8. Apply the locked rule. Append the result to `progress.md`.

## The five things most likely to ruin this

1. **Naming a price before they do.** Voids the WTP data entirely.
2. **Showing Side 2 before asking "did you already know?"** Voids the awareness
   data for that card.
3. **Defending a lead when someone dismisses it.** You are measuring, not
   selling.
4. **Recruiting people who don't win work.** Polite, uninformative, and you have
   burned the leads.
5. **Reading the numbers before the 14-day window closes.**

## Cost

Printing. Five conversations. Two weeks of patience.
