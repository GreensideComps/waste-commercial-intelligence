# Evidence Cards — Participant-Facing

**Print one card per page. Nothing in this file reveals our scoring, our
assumptions about NRS's knowledge, or any revenue figure.**

## How each card is used

Every card has two halves, and **the fold matters**:

- **SIDE 1 — the facts.** Hand this over first. Ask awareness questions
  (Q1/Q2) on Side 1 alone.
- **SIDE 2 — relevance and confidence.** Reveal **only after** the participant
  has answered whether they already knew about it.

Showing "why this may be relevant to NRS" before asking "did you know?" tells
the participant what answer you want. Print the two sides on one sheet, folded,
or as two sheets. **Do not put them side by side.**

**Labels used on the cards, explained to the participant at the start:**
**FACT** = taken from a named public source · **INFERENCE** = our reasoning from
that fact · **ESTIMATE** = a number we derived.

⚠️ **There are no tonnage figures and no £ values on any card.** We could not
derive them from public sources (`docs/ten-leads-analysis.md` §D). Inventing
them would corrupt the test and destroy your credibility in the room.
