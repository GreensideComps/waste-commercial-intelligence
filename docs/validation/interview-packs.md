# Interview Packs

Five packs, five opportunities each. **Card 7 (Mell Square) is the control and
appears in every pack.** The other nine leads are distributed so each is seen by
two or three participants — which is what makes the unanimity rule in
`decision-rule.md` meaningful.

**Cards 2 and 6 get three viewers deliberately.** They are the two leads that
carry the core hypothesis — obscure *and* substantial (Sinfin A), and obscure
*and* specialist-hazardous (Alpha Anodizing). If the product has a future, it
lives in those two patterns, so they get the most evidence.

**Card 7 appears in a different position in every pack** — position 3, 2, 4, 1,
5 — so the control is not always encountered at the same point in the
conversation.

## Rules for running a pack

1. **Do not show the pack as a list.** Hand over one card at a time, face down,
   and complete all nine questions before producing the next. Seeing the set
   first invites comparison and ranking, which is not what we are measuring.
2. **Do not change the order** once printed. The order is already shuffled; if
   you reorder in the room you lose the ability to check for position effects.
3. **Side 2 stays folded** until Q1 and Q2 are answered and written down.
4. **If a participant asks "how many have you got?"** — say ten, and that they
   are seeing five. Do not say which are considered strongest.
5. **Do not run two packs with the same person.** Contamination is immediate and
   irreversible.

## Participant recruitment

Aim for five people, of whom **at least three must be directly involved in
winning work**. A useful spread:

| Slot | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Commercial director / sales lead | The buyer persona |
| P2 | Business development / estimator | Does the work daily |
| P3 | Operations or technical manager | Reality-checks whether NRS could service it |
| P4 | Second commercial/BD person | Independent read on the same leads |
| P5 | Whoever currently does opportunity research | The person the product replaces or assists |

⚠️ **If you cannot find three people involved in winning work, stop.** A test run
with operations staff will produce polite, uninformative answers and you will
have burned the leads.
